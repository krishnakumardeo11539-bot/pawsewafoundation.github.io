ABOUT PAWSEWA

A kinder street starts with us.

PAWSEWA FOUNDATION is a not-for-profit Section 8 company focused on humane community support for dogs. We believe caring for animals and building compassionate communities go hand in hand.

Our approach is simple: feed, care, respond and encourage people to participate.
